Rework the solar-wind flow tracking interface: ``punchbowl.level3.velocity.track_velocity`` now requires a ``product_code`` argument, takes an optional ``reference_time`` on which the flow window is centered, replaces ``ycens`` and ``rbands`` with ``annuli_centers_rs`` and ``annuli_width_rs``, replaces ``delta_t`` (in frames) with ``delta_t_minutes`` (default 64 min), converted to frames for each product, and adds parameters controlling the tracking window and preprocessing (``flow_window_hours``, ``delta_px``, ``expected_wind_kps``, ``offset_speed_kps``, ``vel_bin_width``, ``speed_max``, ``time_avg_hours``, ``use_median``, ``rotate90``, ``remove_temporal_median``, ``deflicker``, ``hdu``). Generating a level-3 VAM now requires enough input files to cover the tracking window of at least 6 hours plus a temporal-average buffer of ``time_avg_hours`` (about 180 PTM frames with default settings), with missing frames interpolated within per-product limits. The pipeline schedules VAMs at the frames nearest to 03:00, 09:00, 15:00 and 21:00 UTC, and punchpipe configurations should rename the ``ycens`` key to ``annuli_centers_rs`` and replace ``delta_t`` with ``delta_t_minutes``.
