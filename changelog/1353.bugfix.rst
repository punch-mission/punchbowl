Fix ``subtract_starfield_background_task`` reprojecting the starfield models into the image frame before handing them to ``remove_starfield``. That extra reprojection did not undo the flux scaling applied when the models were built, and it resampled the models more times than ``remove_starfield`` blurs the image to match. Each model is now passed to ``Starfield.subtract_from_image`` in its own celestial frame and the two subtracted results are interpolated in time. Also fix the starfield uncertainty being subtracted from, rather than combined with, the image uncertainty, and fix polarized images not being converted to the celestial polarization frame before subtraction.
